SnapSet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

SnapSet collects nothing. Your workouts, photos and settings stay on your iPhone. SnapSet has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing over the internet.

What SnapSet stores, and where

Everything you log (exercise names, weights, reps, the time of each set, muscle and equipment tags, and any photos you take of a weight) is stored only on your iPhone, inside SnapSet. It is included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, the same as other apps' data, and nowhere else. SnapSet never adds photos to your photo library.

SnapSet also keeps small diagnostic notes ("receipts") about what it read and whether you corrected it. They contain no photos, handwriting or exercise names, stay on your iPhone, and are removed automatically after 90 days.

Camera, microphone and speech

The camera reads the weight on your equipment. The microphone and speech recognition are used only after you tap the microphone button to say an exercise name. All reading of photos, handwriting and speech happens on your iPhone; nothing is sent to Apple or anyone else for processing.

Sharing

Information leaves your iPhone only when you choose to send it with the share button: a day or week summary, the spreadsheet export, a monthly report, or the diagnostics file. It goes only where you send it.

Purchases

SnapSet Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; SnapSet never sees your payment details and only checks with Apple, on your iPhone, whether Pro is unlocked.

Children

SnapSet does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete any exercise in the app, clear diagnostics in Settings › Diagnostics, or delete SnapSet from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.
